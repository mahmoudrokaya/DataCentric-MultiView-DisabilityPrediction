# Data access and redistribution review

- **NHANES:** Refer to the CDC/NCHS official NHANES data and documentation.
- **HRS:** Refer to the University of Michigan Health and Retirement Study data access terms.
- Verify each included raw/processed dataset's access and redistribution conditions **before public upload**.
- Review serialized models and tabular outputs for privacy and confidentiality concerns.
- If redistribution is not authorized, remove those assets from the public release and supply
  download/reconstruction instructions plus checksums and provenance instead.
- The file inventory does not distinguish public-use, restricted-use, or derived records reliably.

Data sources: https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/ and https://hrs.isr.umich.edu/
